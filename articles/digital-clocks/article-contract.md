## Buying-guide factors

### How to choose a bedroom digital clock

Target: 250–350 words.

1. Minimum brightness and whether the display can turn fully off
2. Alarm volume, snooze, and control access in the dark
3. What backup batteries actually power
4. Digit size at the bed-to-clock viewing distance
5. Whether charging, projection, audio, or smart features justify added light and complexity

### How to choose an office digital clock

Target: 220–300 words.

1. Desk distance versus across-room distance
2. Time-first display versus date, temperature, and humidity clutter
3. Desk footprint, wall mounting, and power-cord routing
4. Manual setting versus auto DST or connected time
5. Offline simplicity versus smart features and privacy/account requirements

### How to choose a classroom digital clock

Target: 250–350 words.

1. Back-row readability and verified digit size
2. Safe wall placement, outlet availability, and cord routing
3. Current-time display versus countdown timer
4. Date/day and 12/24-hour needs
5. Remote controls, setting retention, and daylight-saving adjustments

Buying-guide prose must remain pre-purchase guidance. Do not turn it into setup, maintenance, classroom-management, or sleep advice.
