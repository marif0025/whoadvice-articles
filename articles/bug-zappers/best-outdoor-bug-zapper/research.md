## Eligibility and ranking method

Required gates: exact model, explicit outdoor use, mechanism, exact manual/instructions, outdoor power and placement, current US route, maintenance/parts, identity conflicts, and CPSC result. Advertised acreage, grid voltage, target-insect lists, review stars, and brand fame cannot rescue a failed gate.

Private later-contract weights: exact identity/manual 20%; outdoor placement and power 20%; mechanism/job fit 15%; serviceable parts and cleaning 15%; weather evidence 10%; ownership burden 10%; current availability 5%; evidence quality 5%.

## SERP and reader-decision findings

Editorial competitors consistently emphasize acreage and voltage, yet current evidence does not support treating either as real-world mosquito or bite-control performance. WhoAdvice should lead with outdoor power access, placement away from gathering areas, weather/manual limits, servicing, and whether the reader actually needs a grid, fan trap, or a mosquito-specific alternative.

## Recall, regulatory, and claim controls

- CPSC exact-model/category searches recorded in the five current product files found no matching recall as of July 21–22, 2026; do not publish `recall-free`.
- Conventional UV-only grid kills do not establish fewer bites or a reduced mosquito population.
- `Weather-resistant`, IPX4, ETL/UL, EPA registration/establishment, voltage, and acreage require exact attribution and must not become blanket safety or efficacy conclusions.
- Verify extension-cord and receptacle language from each exact manual before drafting setup advice.
